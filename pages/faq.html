FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about our jerseys, shipping, and ordering process.

Orders & Products

What quality are the jerseys?+
All our jerseys are Thailand AAA grade — the highest quality replica available. They feature correct badge embroidery, fonts, numbers, and fabric weight. These match official jerseys closely in appearance.
Do you have all player names and numbers?+
Most popular players are available ready-made. For less common players or custom numbers, contact us via WhatsApp or email and we can source the specific jersey within 3–5 days.
Do you sell home, away, and third kits?+
Yes — for most clubs we carry home, away, and third/alternate kits. Availability varies by club. We update with new season kits as soon as they are released.
Can I order a jersey not listed?+
Absolutely. We have access to a much wider catalog. Contact us with the jersey you need and we'll confirm availability and pricing within 24 hours.

Sizing

How do I find the right size?+
Check our Size Guide page for a full measurement chart. Asian sizing runs slightly smaller than European/US sizing. If between sizes, go one size up. Message us with your measurements for personal advice.
What sizes do you stock?+
We stock S, M, L, XL, XXL, and XXXL for most jerseys. Some popular items also come in XS and 4XL. Check the product page or contact us to confirm before ordering.

Shipping & Delivery

How long does delivery take?+
Standard delivery: EU 8–12 days, US/Canada 10–15 days, Middle East 7–12 days, Asia Pacific 8–14 days. Exact times depend on your location and customs processing.
Do you ship worldwide?+
Yes — we ship to 100+ countries. If unsure whether we ship to your country, contact us and we'll confirm. Some remote locations may have extended delivery times.
Will I be charged customs duties?+
We declare packages at low value to minimise customs charges. Most customers receive orders without customs fees, but we cannot guarantee this as it varies by country.
What if my package is lost or seized?+
In the rare event a package is lost or seized, we will reship your order free of charge. Contact us with your tracking details and we'll sort it immediately.

Returns & Payments

What payment methods do you accept?+
We accept Visa, Mastercard, Amex, and cryptocurrency (Bitcoin, USDT). Contact us if you have a preferred payment method.
Can I return a jersey?+
Yes. If the jersey arrives damaged, wrong item, or significantly different from described, we offer a full refund or free replacement. For sizing issues, exchange within 14 days of delivery.
