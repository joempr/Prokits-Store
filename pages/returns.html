Our Policy

RETURNS & EXCHANGES

We want you to be 100% satisfied. Here's how our returns and exchange process works.

When Can You Return?

  • Wrong item received — We sent the wrong jersey, club, or size
  • Damaged on arrival — The jersey arrived with defects or damage
  • Significantly not as described — Major quality issues
  • Wrong size — If ordered within our size guide guidelines

How to Initiate a Return

Step 1: Contact us at support@prokitsstore.com within 14 days of receiving your order.

Step 2: Include your order number, photos of the item, and description of the issue.

Step 3: We'll review and respond within 24 hours with next steps.

Step 4: For approved returns — we'll send a replacement or issue a refund.

Refund Timeline

Approved refunds are processed within 3–5 business days back to your original payment method. Crypto refunds are processed within 24 hours.

Exchanges

For size exchanges, we ship the new size at no extra charge. We'll credit you $5 toward your next order to cover your return postage.

⚠️ Note: We do not accept returns for change of mind. Please double-check your order before confirming payment.

✅

14-Day Window

You have 14 days from delivery to contact us about any issues.

🔄

Free Replacements

If the fault is on our side, we reship your correct order completely free of charge.

💳

Full Refunds

For approved refund cases, we process the full amount back to your original payment method.

📸

Photo Evidence

For all return claims, photograph the item clearly so we can process your case quickly.
