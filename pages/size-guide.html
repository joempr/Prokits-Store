How to Measure

Measure your chest at the widest point, keeping the tape measure level. Measure in a relaxed standing position.

🤔

Not Sure?

Send us your chest and height via WhatsApp or email and we'll recommend the perfect size personally.

↕️

Size Up Rule

If between two sizes, always go up. A slightly looser jersey always looks better than one that's too tight.
